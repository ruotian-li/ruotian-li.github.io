About

Ruotian (Kelly) Li

I’m an urban planner based in London, currently studying for an MPlan in City Planning at UCL.

I completed a Bachelor of Urban Planning (Honours) at the University of Auckland, graduating with Upper Second-Class Honours. My strongest modules were in social issues, urban policy analysis, planning theory and sustainable urbanism, and my dissertation explored how psychology could be built into transport policy to help Aucklanders move beyond the car.

My experience spans research, fieldwork and practice. As a Summer Research Scholar, I studied housing market resilience and transit-oriented development around Auckland’s railway stations. With the China Academy of Urban Planning and Design, I carried out village surveys in Jiusuo. In practice, I’ve worked on rebuild projects, subdivision proposals and resource consent applications in Auckland.

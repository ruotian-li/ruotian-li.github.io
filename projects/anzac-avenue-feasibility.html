Sheet 06 · Development feasibility

A mixed-use scheme at 31 Anzac Avenue

A development feasibility appraisal for a vacant city-centre site near Britomart: from planning controls and market evidence to a concept scheme, cost plan and discounted cash flow.

Year
2023
Context
University of Auckland, research project
Role
Individual project
Site
372 m², Business – City Centre Zone, freehold
Budget
$15 million
Methods
Market analysis, cost estimate, NPV, IRR and benefit–cost ratio

In shortThe scheme

Six storeys of small, flexible office suites over ground-floor retail, a short walk from Britomart station.

6 storeys21.3 m, well within the 50 m height limit
2,232 m²gross floor area
74.4 m²average office suite, with no on-site parking
$11.6mestimated cost against a $15m budget

01Site and market

The 372 m² vacant site lies in the Business – City Centre Zone, surrounded by cafés, restaurants, offices and apartments. Market evidence from 2023 showed office vacancy rising while retail vacancy fell — which shaped a scheme of small, flexible office suites over active ground-floor uses. Parking is left out altogether, given the site’s access to public transport.

02Costs and returns

Costs were built up from Rawlinsons construction rates, adjusted for inflation, to a total of about $11.6 million — inside the $15 million budget. Cash flows were tested at several discount rates over 15, 18 and 20-year holding periods, with a 15-year hold giving the best balance of return and risk and an internal rate of return of about 13%.

Final NPV and ROI figures to be confirmed before publishing.

Site data: Auckland Council GeoMaps. Market data: JLL. Construction rates: Rawlinsons.
